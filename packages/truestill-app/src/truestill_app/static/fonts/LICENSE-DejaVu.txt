Truestill bundles the DejaVu Sans Mono typefaces in this directory:

    DejaVuSansMono.ttf        DejaVu Sans Mono, Book (400)
    DejaVuSansMono-Bold.ttf   DejaVu Sans Mono, Bold (700)

Both are DejaVu version 2.37, shipped BYTE-FOR-BYTE AS RELEASED UPSTREAM. They are not
subset, re-encoded, renamed, hinted, or modified in any way. Verify with:

    sha256  a54dca07c76d6289e717e75e0a58c0128f6d7269ef3faf76417c9d7d3bba37ab  DejaVuSansMono.ttf
    sha256  2d67eed9b325ee2e69f906ce4f3f6264d5105230a13352c8494daad4f17b12c8  DejaVuSansMono-Bold.ttf

Upstream: https://dejavu-fonts.github.io/

WHY THIS FILE IS HERE AND NOT IN brand/. The licence condition below binds "all copies of
one or more of the Font Software typefaces". brand/ is not packaged - a built wheel carries
truestill_app/static/* and nothing else - so a notice there would stay behind while the
typefaces travelled. Beside the fonts, it is included in every copy by construction.

WHY NO GLYPH IS MODIFIED. Vera permits modification only if the fonts are renamed to names
containing neither "Bitstream" nor "Vera". We do not rely on that permission: nothing is
altered, so the clause is not reached. (Subsetting would be a modification. It was ruled out
for a different reason - DejaVu was chosen for its 3,322-codepoint coverage, and subsetting
would trade away the thing that won - but it also keeps us clear of this clause.)

DejaVu itself is the renamed Vera derivative that clause contemplates: "DejaVu Sans Mono"
contains neither reserved word.
