How to organize
Look inside first to see what is in the folder.

Trips & events

A multi-day run of camera photos is grouped into one TRIP; a single busy day is its own EVENT. Split apart anything grouped wrong, or merge two runs that should have been one, then name what you want to keep.

Import

Bring in a folder of photos, or the archives any service gave you - a phone export, an old backup, a shared folder. Truestill recovers the real photo dates and removes duplicates.

Backups

Keep your photos safe in more than one place. Copy your library to a second drive, and check your backups stay healthy.
