## 1. The week's defect classes

Not a list of fixes. These are the **shapes that recurred**, each with what to do about it.

### A guard that resolves and does nothing

The most expensive class, because it looks like coverage. Instances this month: the push gate
**inert since `a173c42`**; the retention policy inert under `xdist`; `(aey)`'s census shaped like
the code it was auditing; `_declared()` reading one call shape and blind to `jobs.claim`;
`_EXPECTED` omitting the very route `(agu)` had fixed; the record-state table **passing when
emptied**, because its loop iterated the declaration.

**DO: loop the DERIVED inventory and assert into the DECLARATION**, never the reverse - that is
`ENGINEERING_STANDARD.md` §4's seventy-second member and it is the single highest-value habit here.
**And prove the guard bites**: `scripts/mutate_once.py` for one proof, `mutation_matrix.py` for a
suite. A guard shipped without a mutation is a guard nobody has seen fail.

### A premise true when written and false when acted on

**Ten this month.** One was filed **nine days after its own fix, by the person who fixed it**. One
was **overtaken by a command that shipped the next day** - `(abm)` said three facts reached nobody;
`truestill rescan` shipped 2026-08-07 naming all three, better, and the entry sat open eighteen
days. Another - `(ahr)` - falsified a *founding* rule in `decisions-on-drive-research.md`, and was
put back the same day once the product was repaired.

**DO: verify every premise against today's code before acting on it, including your own from last
week.** `BACKLOG.md`'s absence-claim convention exists for this: a negative claim names the command
that would falsify it and what it returned. The cheapest habit in this repo.

### A test constructing its input differently from the real caller

`(ahp)`: `test_ingest_archives_cli.py` passed a `Path` where argparse hands a `str`, so it proved
the helper works on an input the product never gives it. **Every archive ingest crashed** while
that test stayed green - and stayed green through the fix's own mutation, which is how we know it
never could have caught it.

**DO: for anything reachable from a command line, drive `main()` end to end.** The argparse
boundary is where `Any` enters this program; mypy sees nothing across it.

### An objection recorded against a construct nobody proposed

`jobs.py` justified `JobTarget = Any` against *"a shared **intersection** type jobs cannot enforce
across every target"*. Correct about an intersection - and nobody had suggested one. A
**parameterised** alias claims nothing shared, and eleven factories took their own summary type
with mypy proving each.

**DO: when a comment refuses a design, check that the design it refuses is the one you are
proposing.** A recorded objection is evidence about the past, not a ruling on your idea.
