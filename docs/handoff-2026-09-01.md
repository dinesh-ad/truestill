## 3. Three rulings worth knowing before you touch the neighbourhood

- **`st_dev` can falsify and can never confirm.** Same device **proves** not-independent; different
  device proves nothing. Hence three states, never a boolean - `drive.CopyIndependence`, on
  `drive.DriveReach`'s precedent that *"both folds lie"*.
- **A content hash beside a citation was refused on a measurement**, not a preference: 83 of 220
  cited symbols changed body in twelve days. Symbols, not hashes; **records keep line numbers**,
  and that is a decision.
- **Album membership merges by UNION, not first-wins**, and only because it is append-only - two
  `INSERT OR IGNORE` writers, zero `DELETE`. ⚠ **If a remove ever ships, `_merge_albums` must be
  revisited in the same commit.**
