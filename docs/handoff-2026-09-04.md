## 1. The UI, which is where the work is - and where it is not

**The restyle arc was scoped wrong, ran four commits, and is stopped.** It was built to refine the
existing design against eight targets derived *from* that design, so it kept correctly finding the
design already met them. P218's own report is the evidence: *"they look almost identical, and that
is the correct outcome."*

**The cause was a misreading that is worth stating plainly**: `.scratch/organize-preview/` was
treated as a reference to borrow from. It is the **specification** - the maintainer's words,
*"just a style of design, how it looks, that's it"*. Its live source is committed at
[`research/organize-preview/`](research/organize-preview/); the folder itself is gitignored and
144 MB.

**Then `docs/design-system.md` was written and Organize was built against it. The verdict was
"the old app with a warm background", and that is accurate.** The specification covers **material**
- canvas, surfaces, contrast floors - and **not composition**. `design-system.md` §0 now says so at
the top, so the next session does not build a second screen against it and get the same result.

**The six gaps, measured rather than described** (full table and costs in
[`react-migration-plan.md`](react-migration-plan.md)):

| # | gap | needs | where |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | mode options are native radio dots vs full-width cards with circular icon badges | markup + icons | cutover |
| 2 | **0 `<svg>` across all seven screens**, against 8 in the rail | markup + icons | cutover |
| 3 | the rail's mark is hidden - `.brand-monogram { display: none; }` | **CSS** | **now** |
| 4 | heading is `--type-2xl` (24->27px) `--accent-strong`, vs ~40px near-black | **CSS**, but see below | **blocked** |
| 5 | the panel is a text list vs a dashboard with numerals, a pill and an amber count | JS - `app.js:renderRestingPanel` | cutover |
| 6 | the rail's alert is `▫ ▫ ▫` and prose vs a card with a warning triangle | markup + icons | cutover |

⚠ **One correction to how this was described**: the rail is **not light**. `--rail-bg: #17150f`
and `test_rail_shell.py` asserts it dark in both themes. It is *warm* dark against the preview's
neutral; the visible difference is the missing mark.

⚠ **Item 4 is blocked by a guard, not by the cutover.**
`test_shared_pattern.py::test_only_the_metric_uses_the_metric_size` asserts
`users == [".metric-value"]` and gives its reason - the metric must outrank what it leads. **The
preview disagrees with that ruling. Resolving it is Ad's, not a session's.**

**What survives from the four commits**: the hit-target floor on all seven screens, `(ajm)`'s fix

## 2. What shipped today

high-frequency checkerboard: `spread 229 -> 229` on WebKit, `229 -> 1` on Chromium, headed and
headless alike. So **`@supports (backdrop-filter: ...)` is a guard that cannot fire**, and
`ci.yml` records the stake - *"WebKit is what the Tauri shell renders in on Linux and macOS"*.
`design-system.md` is **solid-first** because of it, which makes the gap free.

it unblocked is the most useful thing here**: `make e2e -n auto` is **5:15** against 26 minutes
serial, so a screen verifies locally and CI confirms nightly.
